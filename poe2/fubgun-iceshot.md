## Campaign Options

- Act 2 - Medallion: Increased charm charges gained
- Act 3 - Venom reward: Stun threshold
- Act 4 - Tattoo: Dex/Cold resist/Fire resist
- Act 4 - Prison: Life recovery from flask
- Interlude - Resist, Movement Speed, or Global Defense
- Act 4 - Shark Fin: 30% evasion/armour/energy shield
