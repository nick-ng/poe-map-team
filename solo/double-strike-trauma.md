## Crafting

### Helm

1. Start with fractured base, reforge defense in harvest until you have 2 t1 or
   t2 mods (you can decide if you want to settle for t2), we are looking to
   %armour/es, flat armour/es, and life. If you get 2 prefix that you want with
   a third prefix, annul and pray.
2. Once you have 2 of the prefix either t1 or t2 and an open prefix, take the
   helm to the boat and allflame exalt, I did foulborn exalt and got it on my
   first try, but I am not sure if I just got lucky or what the expected value
   of using foulborn exalt over normal exalt. If you miss, yolo anul and pray.
   Repeat this process until you have 3 prefixs you want.
3. Prefix cant be changed then reforge chaos on harvest bench. Repeat until t1
   chaos res. If you fill suffix then eldrich annul and repeat.
4. Craft aspect of lunaris by taking your helm to allflame and using brinehook
   ducat. (you need an open suffix).

### Gloves

1. Start with fractured base
2. Use attack speed ess until you have attack speed and an empty suffix, then
   suffix cant change reforge chaos. I think this is cheaper than spamming ess
   until attack speed AND t1 chaos res. At the time I crafted the gloves it was
   11 ess to 1div. If they are cheaper you can just ess spame.
3. Eldrich craft prefix, start with empty prefix, craft mana to block, exalt, if
   you miss remove crafted mana and eldrich annul. If you hit t1/t2 prefix then
   keep crafted mana on as a block and take to the boat to all flame exalt slam.
   If you miss remove craft and eldrich annul and hope you hit bad mod, if you
   hit bad mod try again, if you miss annul then remove all prefix and start
   again. This part is quite painful. Do this until you have t1/t2 %armour/es
   and t1/t2 life.
4. Put on eldrich implicits and conflict strike additional targets to +2, you
   can use conflicts with allflame crafting.

### Shield

1. Start with fractured base (get flat fractured if you can, not % like I have)
   You use envy ess spam until t2 or higher crit reduction and an empty suffix,
   then suffix cant change reforge phys until t2 or higher PDR.
2. Suffix cant be change reforge life until t1/t2 (not sure if this is the best
   way), then craft last mod.
